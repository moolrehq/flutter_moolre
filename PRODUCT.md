# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Flutter developers integrating Moolre and customers paying Ghanaian merchants with mobile money on their phones.

## Product Purpose

Complete mobile-money checkout inside a Flutter interface, with phone approval and verified payment outcomes. The accepted native checkout plan replaces WebView and browser redirects.

## Brand Personality

Clear, dependable, familiar. Preserve the existing Moolre logo and navy identity, and inherit the consuming application's Material theme.

## Anti-references

Browser checkout, decorative payment animations, and success screens shown before payment verification.

## Design Principles

- Show the payment amount before asking for approval.
- Explain the next action clearly, especially OTP and phone approval.
- Keep pending payments distinct from failures and dismissed screens.
- Return results without changing the merchant application's navigation stack.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use standard Material form controls, visible labels, readable contrast, keyboard-safe scrolling, text scaling, and labelled payment status changes. Convey state in text as well as icons and colors.
